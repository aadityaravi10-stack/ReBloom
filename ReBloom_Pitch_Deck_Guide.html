Slide 2 • What Makes ReBloom Out of the League

4 Pillars Separating ReBloom From Generic Job Boards Architected specifically for psychological safety

1. 🧭 30-60-90 Structured Flightpath

Unlike regular job postings that say 'flexible work', every ReBloom cohort includes a contractual 3-phase ramp-up:

  • Month 1 (Days 1–30): Sandbox labs, tooling refreshers & 1-on-1 mentor shadowing.
  • Month 2 (Days 31–60): Agile squad impact and pair-programming on live features.
  • Month 3 (Days 61–90): Feature ownership and structured full-time conversion evaluation.

2. 🛡️ Empathy Scorecard & Core Hours

Employers must publish their Psychological Safety commitments upfront:

  • Core Focus Window: Predictable meeting hours (e.g. 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM IST) for school pickup safety.
  • Caregiver Emergency Leave: 5 paid emergency days for child/parent health.
  • Executive Sponsorship: Director-level champions to overcome mid-career bias.

3. 🤖 AI Career Gap Reframer

Proprietary generative AI persona trained on empathetic talent acquisition:

  • Transforms multi-year caregiving breaks into executive resumes highlighting crisis management, budgeting, and adaptability.
  • Optimizes resume bullet points specifically for returnship hiring committees.

4. 💎 Complete Transparency (Zero Truncation)

No vague salary bands or hidden terms:

  • Guaranteed stipends explicitly stated (e.g. ₹1,40,000 / month).
  • Exact historical PPO full-time conversion rate (88% to 92%).
  • Clear accepted break thresholds (1+, 2+, or 3+ years).
